ShikshaGap

A diagnostic math assessment for Class 5 students that pinpoints foundational prerequisite gaps instead of just grading right or wrong.

Note: Brand-new codebase written completely from scratch in this repository for this hackathon, with no code reused from the learner's earlier project.

🎯 The Unique Kernel

When a student gets a question wrong, the quiz doesn't give them another question of the same type—it steps backward down the concept prerequisite chain to locate the foundational skill where understanding broke down.

3-Level Chain: Unlike Fractions (Level 3) → Equivalent Fractions (Level 2) → Multiplication Facts (Level 1).

👤 Who It's For & Why

Audience: A Class 5 student working independently on a phone or laptop.

The Problem: Traditional tests stamp a low score on fractions without explaining the root cause. Parents and students are left discouraged.

Motivation: Inspired by the memory of how frustrating school math was when falling behind without understanding where the gap was, and wanting to help students catch foundational gaps early to stay confident.

📊 Core Adaptive Diagnostic Loop

🛡️ Scope Boundaries

  • Single Math Topic: Adding fractions with different denominators.
  • 3-Level Diagnostic Hierarchy: Unlike Fractions (L3) → Equivalent Fractions (L2) → Multiplication Facts (L1).
  • Curated Question Bank: Static, pre-authored questions with difficulty and prerequisite tags.
  • Deterministic Adaptive Engine: Steps up on correct, steps down to prerequisite on incorrect. Terminates at gap pinpoint or ~6 questions.
  • Shuffled Answer Choices: 4 options per question with randomized positions so the correct answer isn't always 'A'.
  • Diagnostic Result Screen: Names identified gap, explains the diagnostic reasoning trail, and gives one actionable practice next-step.
  • Teacher & Parent Dashboards: Class aggregate reports and progress summaries.
  • Printable Practice Worksheets: Downloadable PDF exercises matching diagnosed gaps.
  • Multi-Topic Expansion: Decimals, long division, geometry, and multiple grade levels.
  • User Profiles & Persistence: Saving historical attempts across student logins.
  • AI-Generated Variations: Dynamic generation for endless unique quizzes.
  • Cloud Database (Supabase): Cut to avoid backend network latency, configuration hurdles, and silent write bugs. In-memory state guarantees determinism.
  • User Authentication: Cut to enable zero-friction, one-click access for the 60-second hackathon demo.
  • Hindi & Telugu Translations: Cut to keep content curation tightly focused on English math terminology during the PoC.
  • AI-Generated Questions at Runtime: Cut to prevent model hallucinations, syntax delays, or unpredictable test answers.
  • Worksheet Generation: Cut to keep the post-quiz experience focused on clear diagnostic feedback.
